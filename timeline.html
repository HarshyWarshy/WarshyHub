Timeline Review

Quick Era Timeline for AP World History

Major Eras

1200–1450

Global trade networks expanded across Afro-Eurasia through the Silk Roads, Indian Ocean routes, and trans-Saharan exchange.

1450–1750

Land-based empires and maritime exploration connected continents through conquest, commerce, and Columbian Exchange transformations.

1750–1900

Industrialization reshaped labor, migration, and empire as revolutions and reforms spread alongside new political ideas.

1900–Present

World wars, decolonization, Cold War tensions, and globalization drove rapid change in states, societies, and economies.
