Timeline Mastery

Detailed Period Guide + Evidence You Can Use

1200–1450 1450–1750 1750–1900 1900–Present

1200–1450

Global tapestry + exchange networks; growth in trade, diasporas, and cultural diffusion.

Evidence

  • Mansa Musa
  • Silk Roads caravanserai
  • Indian Ocean trade cities

1450–1750

Gunpowder empires, maritime expansion, Columbian Exchange, and expanding coerced labor systems.

Evidence

  • Ottoman Janissaries
  • Potosí silver
  • Atlantic slave trade

1750–1900

Industrialization, revolution, and imperialism transformed work, migration, and state power.

Evidence

  • Haitian Revolution
  • Meiji Restoration
  • Berlin Conference

1900–Present

World wars, decolonization, Cold War alignments, and globalization reshaped modern systems.

Evidence

  • Non-Aligned Movement
  • UN institutions
  • Digital globalization
