Study Systems

Tools for Better Retention and Faster Writing

1) 20-Min Unit Sprint

  • 5 min: skim one unit section.
  • 7 min: write 8 bullet facts from memory.
  • 8 min: answer one prompt from Practice page.

2) LEQ Outline Template

  • Context sentence
  • Thesis with line of reasoning
  • Body claim 1 + evidence + explanation
  • Body claim 2 + evidence + explanation

3) Mistake Log

  • What prompt did you miss?
  • Was the issue knowledge, argument, or evidence?
  • What one fix will you apply next attempt?
