In-Depth Weekly Plan

DayMain GoalDetailed Work
MondayPeriod contentRead one timeline section, create 12 fact bullets, then write a 3-sentence summary using chronology words (before/after/therefore).
TuesdayTheme connectionPick one event and connect it to at least two themes. Build one comparison sentence and one causation sentence.
WednesdayVideo reinforcementWatch one Heimler unit review and pause to annotate evidence examples you could use in a DBQ.
ThursdayPrompt repsUse the practice page for 3 prompts. Do 2-minute outlines and 6-minute mini responses.
FridaySynthesisWrite one mini-LEQ paragraph combining context, claim, two pieces of evidence, and reasoning.
WeekendReview + gapsRevisit weak units, retake one prompt, and update your own error log (missing context? vague evidence? weak thesis?).
