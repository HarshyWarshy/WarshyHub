3-Round Practice Method

Round 1: Outline (5 min)

  • Identify command term + time range.
  • Write thesis in one sentence.
  • Add three evidence bullets.

Round 2: Write (8–12 min)

  • Keep one paragraph per claim.
  • Use specific names and dates/periods where possible.
  • Explain why evidence proves your claim.

Round 3: Reflect (3 min)

  • Mark weak evidence spots.
  • Rewrite thesis for clarity.
  • Log one improvement for next rep.
